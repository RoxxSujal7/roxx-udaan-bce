ABOUT US

UDAAN 2026 is the annual sports fest of Bhagalpur College of Engineering (BCE), Bhagalpur, organized by the UDAAN Sports Club from 27th to 30th November 2026. Established in 1960 and affiliated to Bihar Engineering University (BEU), Patna, BCE functions under the Department of Science, Technology & Technical Education, Government of Bihar.

Set on the Sabour campus along the banks of the Ganga in Bhagalpur, Bihar – 813210, UDAAN celebrates teamwork, resilience, and campus spirit. Guided by our theme, "Journey from efforts to excellence," the fest gives students a vibrant arena to test their discipline and passion.

Across four action-packed days, UDAAN 2026 features twelve diverse events spanning Cricket, Football, Badminton, Table Tennis, Volleyball, Athletics, Board Games, Javelin, Kabaddi, and Tug of War, alongside Literary and Cultural competitions that bring the entire campus together.

Train hard, step onto the field, and give your best at UDAAN 2026—where every effort becomes a stride toward excellence.

A MESSAGE FROM

THE PRINCIPAL'S DESK

Dr. Bimlesh Kumar

Principal, Bhagalpur College of Engineering

Bhagalpur College of Engineering, established in 1960 along the banks of the Ganga in Sabour, Bhagalpur, has long been a hub of holistic student development. With the UDAAN Sports Club organizing UDAAN 2026 from the 27th to the 30th of November, I am delighted to see our students come together on the field and stage. Spanning sports alongside literary and cultural events, UDAAN reflects the discipline, teamwork, and perseverance that engineering education stands for. True to its theme, "Journey from efforts to excellence," I am confident that every participant will compete with integrity and rise to the occasion.

I extend my best wishes to the organizers and the participants for a memorable UDAAN 2026.
