A MESSAGE FROM

THE PRINCIPAL'S DESK

Dr. Bimlesh Kumar

Principal, Bhagalpur College of Engineering

Established in 1960 and now affiliated to Bihar Engineering University (BEU), Patna under the Department of Science, Technology & Technical Education, Government of Bihar, Bhagalpur College of Engineering has long stood for academic rigor and all-round student growth. Situated on the Sabour campus along the banks of the Ganga, our institution believes that true engineering education extends far beyond classrooms and laboratories.

Sports and co-curricular pursuits play an irreplaceable role in shaping character, resilience, and leadership. On the field, students learn how to handle pressure, support teammates, respect opponents, and bounce back from setbacks—qualities that remain essential throughout life and professional careers.

It gives me immense pleasure to see the UDAAN Sports Club bring the campus together for UDAAN 2026 from 27th to 30th November 2026. With twelve events spanning Cricket, Football, Badminton, Table Tennis, Volleyball, Athletics, Board Games, Javelin, Kabaddi, Tug of War, Literary, and Cultural competitions, the fest offers every student a stage to shine.

Guided by the motto "Journey from efforts to excellence," UDAAN 2026 reminds us that meaningful achievement is built on steady preparation, fair play, and collective spirit. Whether competing for a podium finish or cheering from the sidelines, every member of the BCE community adds energy to this celebration.

I extend my best wishes to the organizers and all participants for a successful and memorable UDAAN 2026.
